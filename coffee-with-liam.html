A little clarity before you begin

A few honest
answers.

Do I need a specific question?

No. A feeling, a repeating pattern, or the sense that something needs attention is enough to begin. You can also explore a reflection in The Mirror first.

Is this therapy?

Coffee with Liam is a reflective conversation. It is not therapy, diagnosis, or a substitute for professional mental health care.

How do I arrange a time?

Prepare your message above and choose “Email this directly,” or email MBNCoach@gmail.com with “Coffee with Liam” and your request. The time and conversation details are arranged by email. This page does not offer confirmed booking slots.

Is there a charge?

The first one hundred hours of these beginning conversations are offered freely, in 30 or 60 minute sessions. There is no payment step on this page. The complimentary offer is subject to availability.

Where does my draft go?

Your draft stays in this open page until you open it in your email app or copy it into an email. It is not automatically saved or submitted. When you send that email, your message goes through your email provider and the recipient’s email service.
